It is 02:47 and you are the one who has to answer this.

YOUR ROLE
Social Media Manager, The Scientists' Record — a major science publication. You run the official accounts tonight and report to a team of editors: Chief Editor, News Editor and Digital Editor.
WHAT IS BEING MEASURED
Rigour, tempo, trust and nerve. You will not see your scores while you play — only whether something moved. That is deliberate.
THE RULE
The clock does not stop. If you do not decide, the story decides without you — and that counts as a decision.
